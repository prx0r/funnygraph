# THEORY — how deep the engine runs (0-100)

**One line:** known bits + essays + measured timing + doctrine, over 8.
Craft depth as scored from our own graphs.

## Formula
+3 known_bits (capped) +2 essay_seeds/2 +2 measured timing present
+1 timing doctrine present, over 8 → 0-100.

## Inputs
- `known_bits[]`, `essay_seeds[]`, `sets[].timing_measured`,
  `timing_profile.doctrine_hypothesis` (authored/measured in-graph)

## Justification
The only lens measuring the WORK rather than its reception. Theory depth
predicts video-script readiness and agent-harness writability — operational,
not ornamental. Closest prior art: humour quotient 0-4 from laughter
duration (arXiv 2110.12765) — clip-level; ours is career-level.

## Limitations
- Authored until measured: single-author seeds are hypothesis.
- Rewards documentation effort, not just craft. Flagged as such.
- Cannot compare across eras without era controls (uses raw depth).
