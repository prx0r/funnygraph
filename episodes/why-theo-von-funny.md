## Cold open (0:00-0:45)
A man from Louisiana explains modern life like it's a foreign country
he's visiting with the wrong map — and the wrong map keeps being right.
[CLIP: Theo riffing on something mundane turning cosmic]
Theo Von shouldn't be analyzable. He sounds like he's making it up. Half
the time he sounds surprised by his own sentences. And he's the most
imitated voice in comedy podcasting right now. Why?

## Act 1 — The engine (0:45-3:00)
**Thesis: the swamp philosopher.** Theo treats modern life as a foreign
country he's reporting from — with the wrong map. The goof-to-wisdom
ratio IS the engine: the room is laughing at the goof when the wisdom
lands, and the wisdom hits harder because they were laughing.

The mechanism, beat by beat:
1. **The drawl.** Slow Louisiana cadence with long pauses before the weird
   image. The room leans in because he sounds like he's not trying —
   and the not-trying is doing all the work.
2. **The wrong map.** Takes a normal thing (airports, possums, childhood)
   and navigates it with backwoods cosmology. Literalize: treat the small
   thing as the whole universe until it is.
3. **The landing.** Sudden precision inside the swamp of words. One clean
   sentence after forty muddy ones — the contrast is the punchline.

Theory links: literalize (animals in people clothes, road stories as
cosmology) + incongruity (swamp logic applied to modern life) +
benign_violation (the violation is how dumb it sounds; the benign frame
is that he's the sweetest guy in the room).
