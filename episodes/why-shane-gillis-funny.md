## Act 1 — The engine (0:45-3:00)
**Thesis: the lovable-idiot-warlord.** Gillis takes every cultural cue at
face value from inside the dumbest plausible reading — then commits so hard
the reading becomes the joke.

The mechanism, beat by beat:
1. **The straight face.** He delivers the dumb take like a weather report.
   No wink. The room believes him for half a second.
2. **The half-second.** That gap — where the audience isn't sure if he's
   serious — IS the joke. Everything else is delivery.
3. **The break.** He cracks up at his own bit. Every acting teacher would
   call this a flaw. It's the opposite: the break is the audience's
   permission structure. He's laughing WITH us at himself.

Theory links: rigidify (applies dumb logic as law) + benign_violation
(the violation is the take; the benign frame is the obvious love) +
status_reversal (the idiot is the freest guy in the room).

## Act 3 — The three bits that prove it (5:00-8:00)
1. **Local advertising voice** — adopts a regional type fully, never winks.
   The room laughs at the type they recognize AND the commitment.
2. **The roast as love language** — mean AND everyone involved is in on it.
   The boys' cruelty with the boys' love as the frame.
3. **Cancellation material** — treats the culture war as guys being wrong
   out loud. The confident-wrongness transfers because the topic was never
   the point.
