# TOP 2026 — critic survey ballot (v1)

> 20+ voters: working comics, club bookers, comedy journalists.
> Published voter list (opt-in named, else anonymous-but-counted).
> Borda + Kemenization, same code as rank_comedians.py.

## Ballot

**Voter:** name (or anonymous ID) · role (comic/booker/journalist) · date

**Q1. Top 20 working comics RIGHT NOW (2026).** Rank 1-20. Anyone active.
*Counts as a full ranking ballot (Borda).*

**Q2. Breakout of the year.** One name who broke through in 2026.
*Counts toward BREAKOUT 2026 board.*

**Q3. Most underrated.** One name the canon missed.
*Counts toward disagreement/underrated analysis.*

**Q4. Best special of 2026.** One title.
*Counts toward specials board + standout flags.*

**Q5 (optional). Pairwise confidence.** "X over Y because ___" — up to 3 pairs.
*Feeds Bradley-Terry leg directly.*

## Rules
- No self-votes (comics can't rank themselves top-5).
- No PR firms, no campaigns. Rate-limited, outlier detection.
- Ballots published (anonymized IDs) with the board. Auditability IS the product.

## Distribution
- Direct outreach: 30 targets (comics with shared history + bookers + 5 journalists).
- Arena app embeds the same ballot (verified voters only).
- Deadline: Nov 30, 2026. Board publishes December.
