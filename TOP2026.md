# TOP COMEDIANS 2026 — how funnygraph becomes definitive

> Not a better average of stale lists. A NEW election, run by us, with
> transparent methodology, timestamped 2026, repeated annually.

## Why we can win this

Nobody runs an open-methodology comedian vote. Ranker is brigaded opaqueness.
Listicles are closed panels. IMDb is per-special, never aggregated. Our edge:
**we publish the voters, the weights, the missing-data rules, and the
disagreement** — and we have something nobody else has: blind pairwise arena
votes (characters repo, Elo/Wilson, n≥30 standard).

## The 2026 election: 4 fresh legs

| Leg | Source | Status |

| **Arena** | blind pairwise battles (our app), Bradley-Terry scores | infra exists, needs n |

Only post-2020-eligible activity counts. Pryor can't win 2026 (he's dead) —
but his *influence rank* can appear as a separate "canon carryover" note.
ALL-TIME and 2026 are different elections; conflating them is the original sin
of every GOAT list.

## Ballot design (arena leg)

- Blind A/B clip battles, 60-90s clips, random pairings across generations
- Wilson score intervals, n≥30 votes before a pairing counts
- Anti-brigading: rate-limited, no campaign links, outlier detection
- Bradley-Terry global scores via Zermelo iteration (Newman 2023)

## Critic survey design

- 20+ voters: working comics, club bookers, comedy journalists
- Each submits a ranked top-20 + pairwise confidence
- Published voter list (opt-in named, else anonymous-but-counted)
- Borda + Kemenization, same code as rank_comedians.py

Not authority — **auditability**. Anyone can rerun the election from our data
and code and get our board. No listicle offers that. The methodology IS the moat.
