## 9. House scoring deep-dive (Norm #1 analysis, 19:15 UTC)

### Why Norm was #1 (v1)
- P2 (craft=87.5) dominated because Norm/Louis are the ONLY comics with
  measured timing — a characters2 team coverage artifact, not craft quality.
- Carlin had higher P1 (0.025 vs 0.0242) but lower P2 (62.5 vs 87.5).
- Under ANY sample-size shrinkage (k=100..1000), Carlin #1, Norm #5-#9.

### Transcript audit
- 161 graphs have transcripts; 173 transcript dirs; 12 orphaned (special-specific names)
- Louis: 11 transcripts in characters2, ZERO locally (theory_content.py reads from c2)
- 254 kings have NO transcripts (Paste/C4 legends drafted but not ingested)
- Norm: 24,744 words; Carlin: 131,013 words — 5x sample difference

### Tests run (9 scoring variants)
- Test 1 (P1 only): Carlin > Seinfeld > Norm
- Test 2 (P2 only): Norm = Louis (documentation bias)
- Test 5 (P2-mod, remove timing): Carlin > Norm > Louis
- Test 8 (shrinkage k=500): Carlin > Rock > Chappelle > C.K. > Lee > Norm
- Conclusion: Norm #1 is a P2 artifact. Under any defensible scoring, Carlin #1.

### Fixes applied
1. P1 shrunk (k=500 Empirical-Bayes): paper * n_bits / (n_bits + 500)
2. P2 capped as modifier (+15% max), never co-equal
3. P2-only comics get NO rank (owner caught Bert at #11 with zero P1)
4. Original rank preserved as `original_rank` for comparison

### Corrected board top 5
1. George Carlin (0.984, n=4478, was#3)
2. Louis C.K. (0.908, n=2761, was#2)
3. Chris Rock (0.883, n=3233, was#4)
4. Dave Chappelle (0.863, n=3432, was#8)
5. Norm Macdonald (0.741, n=1045, was#1)

### Sweep stability (corrected)
Carlin [1,1] — rock solid. Louis [2,4]. Rock [2,3]. Norm [5,6].
Much tighter than v1 sweeps (Norm was [1,3]).
