# Personality assessment — Dave Chappelle (stage persona)

Companion to `comedian-graph.json`. Same five-slot template. Stage
persona only. Framework estimates are hypotheses.

## The read, formalized

No owner one-liner handed; composite from public specials knowledge and
craft reputation (the return from the $50M exit, the Hold Your applause
era, the block-party register):

> the block's designated truth-teller who took the long way around;
> everybody already knows, the comedy is in the SAYING; holds the
> silence after the heavy point until the room does its own indicting;
> the most famous guy in the room performing like the most normal.

## Framework 3 — Archetype cluster

**Primary: The Raconteur-King** — story-form at throne-scale. The
block's truth-teller who happens to have the biggest room. The
rôle combines the neighborhood megaphone with the returning-legend
authority.

**Secondary: The Sermon-Holder** — the altar-call timing doctrine.
Steps out of the story, holds the silence, the room preaches to
itself, he steps back in. The sermon that never calls itself preaching
(Carlin calls it preaching; Chappelle holds the space and lets the
congregation do it).

**Shadow: The Trickster-Quitter** — the exit itself as the bit.
Quit the biggest show, came back to arena rooms, made the quit part
of the legend. The trickster move is玩弄 the status game publicly —
he plays with leaving the way Norm plays with length.

**Explicitly NOT:**
- The Confessor (Louis) — the self-targeting isn't the engine
- The Auditor (Seinfeld) — he's IN the mess, narrating from inside
- The Prophet (Carlin) — never lectures; the room arrives at the
  verdict alone
- The Prosecutor (Rock) — doesn't pace and point; sits back and lets
  the story indict
- The Guardian (Norm) — not defending the FORM; defending the TRUTH
  the form carries

## Framework 4 — Status economics

**Capital: VERY HIGH (0.85).** The quit-and-return mythology IS the
capital. He holds the rarest position: the guy who walked away from
the most and came back owning the decision. The room grants him
legend-status before he opens his mouth — which is why he can spend
the first five minutes on nothing and the room waits.

**Exit strategy: THRONE.** Left the throne, came back to it. The exit
was the flex; the return was the proof. Contrast Norm (held capital
in reserve, never spent it on modification) and Louis (zero capital,
nothing to spend). Chappelle's capital is the exit narrative itself —
he can say the unsayable because he's already demonstrated he'll pay
real costs for honesty.

**License: the quit.** "I walked away from $50M" is the ultimate
transgression-credit. Whatever he says next, the room can't call him
a sellout — he already refused the biggest sale. This is why his TA
can be systems: he's credibly outside the system he's narrating, even
while standing in an arena paid for by it.

## Collisions

**Freaks roster:** WHO cell `throne-exit` — empty. A creature who left
its own world's hierarchy and returned with new authority: a god who
abdicated and came back, a guild-master who quit and was begged back.
Our greek canon (Zeus) is adjacent but never LEFT. A new freak could
occupy the exited-throne cell.

**Fiction:** Atticus Finch (the moral authority the room grants before
he speaks — but Atticus is sincere-prosecutor, not comic); Teal'c
from Stargate (the outsider-insider who narrates the system he served)?
— partial; The Wire's Bunny Colvin (the institution's man who tells
the truth at cost and keeps his seat); NOT Don Draper (the legend
mask without the communal warmth).

**Lineage:** Pryor (confessional fire, block-level truth) → Chappelle
(the fire cooled into story-form at throne-scale). Also: the Black
comedian-as-community-narrator tradition (Rock prosecutes it,
Chappelle holds it). The Talking Funny panel's absence is itself
data — he's the generation AFTER that table, and the one that made
the special-form into an event again.

## One-word persona

**king.** Not "legend" (that's the resume); not "storyteller" (that's
the form); not "truth-teller" (that's the content). The persona is
regal — the room grants the throne and he sits in it while telling
you about the block. Two words if needed: reluctant king.
