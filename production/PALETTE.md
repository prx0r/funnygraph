## PRINCIPLES

1. **Colour = engine.** The colour tells you what kind of comedy before
   a word is spoken. Louis's orange = warm confession. Norm's blue =
   cold stillness. Casey's rainbow = chaos.
2. **Flat, no gradients.** Clean backdrop. The comedian is the subject.
   The colour is the mood.
3. **Face-forward.** Head and shoulders. No full body. The face IS the
   instrument.
4. **White text.** Always. The colour is the backdrop, not the type.
5. **One comedian per frame.** No two faces. The episode is about ONE
   machine.
